### Added
- Agent inbox rows now offer a Close button (except on watching devices, offline PCs, or locked
  pinned agents), hidden until hover or focus on desktop and always shown on touch screens. An open
  agent thread's More menu (on phones and wide windows), the Close pane shortcut, and the command
  palette's Close pane action close the open thread under the same rules: confirming before closing a
  working agent, a blocked agent, or a workspace's last pane, and returning to the agent list.
